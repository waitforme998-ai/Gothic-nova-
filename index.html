STORE POLICIES & LOGISTICS

Cash On Delivery (COD) Parcel Policy

• Delivery Timeline: 5–7 business days nationwide across Pakistan.

• Courier Partner: Dispatched securely via PostEx logistics.

• Tracking: A real-time tracking code & booking slip are shared directly to your WhatsApp upon courier dispatch.

Advance Payment Parcel (Priority Express)

• Delivery Timeline: Express 2–3 business days (Overnight priority dispatch where available in major cities).

• Payment Methods: Direct Bank Transfer (Bank Alfalah) or JazzCash.

• Courier Partners: Dispatched via premier courier networks (PostEx / Trax / Leopards).
