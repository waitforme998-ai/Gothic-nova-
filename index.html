STORE POLICIES & LOGISTICS

Cash On Delivery (COD) Parcel Policy

• Delivery Timeline: 5–7 business days nationwide across Pakistan.

• Advance Verification Deposit: A deposit of Rs. 700 is required prior to dispatch to secure packing materials & courier logistics. The remaining order balance is paid upon doorstep delivery.

• Courier Partner: Dispatched securely via PostEx logistics.

• Tracking: A real-time tracking code is sent via SMS & WhatsApp immediately upon courier booking.

Advance Payment Parcel (Priority Express)

• Delivery Timeline: Express 2–3 business days (Overnight priority dispatch where available in major cities).

• Payment Methods: Direct Bank Transfer, EasyPaisa, or JazzCash.

• Courier Partners: Dispatched via premier accepted couriers (TCS / Leopards / Trax).

• Zero Handling Fee: 100% waives courier COD collection surcharges.

14-Day Exchange & Guarantee

• Hassle-Free Sizing Exchanges: If your ring or bracelet size isn't a perfect fit, initiate an exchange within 14 days of parcel delivery.

• Condition: Items must be returned unworn in their original signature velvet box packaging.

Customer Support & Concierge
