How to Use

GUIDE
Admin Management Controls

✦Top Announcement Bar Editor

Manage the promotional banner that floats above the storefront header across all pages:

  • 1 to 5 Offer Slots: Enter custom promotional lines (flash sales, discount codes, shipping notices).
  • 1 Offer Mode: Renders a crisp, static centered banner without unnecessary animation.
  • 2 to 5 Offers Mode: Automatically creates a smooth, continuous 100% seamless marquee scroll with hover-to-pause capability.
  • 0 Offers (Hide): Clear all 5 inputs and tap Save Announcements to completely hide the banner and adjust the navbar to the top of the screen.

✦Stock Images & Angle Shots

Attach up to 3 extra angle photos or lifestyle shots to any product:

  • Single Product: In the product sheet, tap + Add Photo under Optional Stock Images to attach secondary angle views.
  • Bulk Wizard: Attach angle images to individual products during batch uploads with live thumbnail management.

✦Admin Search & Glowing Jump (/ or Ctrl+K)

  • Global Hotkeys: Tap SEARCH or press / anywhere to search products, categories, or reviews.
  • Instant Focus: Clicking any search result automatically opens that accordion, scrolls to the item, and pulses with a high-visibility crimson glow.

✦Product Creation & Bulk Wizard

  • Single Upload: Selecting 1 photo opens the single-item form to set name, regular price, sale discount, category, description, stock, and low-stock threshold.
  • Bulk Upload: Selecting multiple photos launches the batch naming wizard with automatic draft saving and instant draft recovery banner.

✦Category Management & Safety Guards

  • Add / Rename: Create new categories inline or tap the pencil icon to rename an existing category across the entire store.
  • Deletion Guard: Categories containing active products cannot be deleted until those items are moved or deleted.

✦Customer Reviews & Ratings Engine

  • Manage Feedback: Tap + ADD REVIEW to add customer testimonials with 1 to 5 stars, customer name, and city.
  • Live Sync: Newly added or edited reviews immediately reflect on the public storefront and update the overall rating average badge.

✦Live Inventory Steppers & Low-Stock Alerts

  • Instant Stock Changes: Tap + and − stepper buttons in the category accordion to adjust stock counts with zero reloads.
  • Critical Alerts: When stock hits low threshold (default 3), the red Alerts header card lights up. Click it to view the full list of low-stock and sold-out items.
  • Sold Out Automation: Setting stock to 0 automatically marks the product as Sold Out on the public site and disables buy buttons.
Customer Storefront Experience

✦Quick Product Overlay & Angle Gallery

  • No-Reload Quick View: Clicking any product opens a centered glassmorphic overlay without navigating away from the page.
  • Multi-Angle Thumbnails: Tapping any angle thumbnail switches the view instantly with zero delay.
  • Fullscreen Zoom Lightbox: Clicking the main product image opens a high-resolution fullscreen zoom view with close button and Escape key support.
  • Deep Linking: Direct product URLs (e.g. ?product=2) automatically open the product modal for instant sharing.

✦Expanding Search & Smooth Product Scroll

  • Instant Overlay: Shoppers can press / or tap the search icon to open the full-screen search bar with popular products and category chips.
  • Direct Card Scroll: Clicking any search result smoothly scrolls the customer straight to that product card on the home page and triggers an attention-grabbing glow pulse.

✦Slide-Out Cart Drawer & Micro-Animations

  • Interactive Cart: Displays line items with photo thumbnails, price calculations in PKR (Rs.), and live quantity steppers capped at current stock.
  • Add-to-Cart Feedback: Adding an item triggers a glowing flying orb animation to the cart icon and turns the button into VIEW CART.

✦Dynamic Footer & Category Tabs

  • Synced Collections: Footer collection links automatically match your active store categories.
  • Automatic New Arrivals: The top 10 newest catalog additions are dynamically featured in the New Arrivals showcase.
  • Logistics Policy Popups: Interactive modals in the footer explain Cash on Delivery (COD), Advance Deposit, and Courier Tracking policies.

✦Order Fulfillment & Pricing Structure

Nationwide dispatch and checkout pricing protocol:

  • Bundled Pricing Architecture: Delivery and fulfillment are incorporated directly into the product catalog pricing with zero extra delivery fee add-ons.
  • Direct Product Management: Products only require Price and optional Sale Price without manual delivery fee configuration.
  • Tarot Rewards Integration: Free delivery, discounts, mystery gifts, and VIP upgrades won by customers are automatically documented inside each order.

✦Payment Methods Architecture & Deposit Proofs

Dual-tier checkout payment system designed for maximum conversion and fraud prevention:

  • Full Advance (Pre-selected Priority): 100% full transfer via Bank Makramah (BML) or JazzCash with priority express dispatch.
  • Cash on Delivery (Advance Deposit): Requires a mandatory advance verification deposit of Rs. 700 to eliminate fake/unclaimed orders, with the remaining order balance paid at doorstep upon delivery.
  • Payment Proof Verification: Customers attach transfer receipt screenshots during checkout, reviewed and verified directly in the Admin Orders list.

✦Sacred 48-Hour Tarot Ritual & Reward Redemptions

Automated customer engagement and conversion catalyst:

  • 48-Hour Strict Lock: First-time visitors draw 1 sacred card from the Gothic Tarot deck with a strict 48-hour countdown timer stored in localStorage.
  • Reward Types: Includes Free Delivery (waives shipping and COD deposit), 10% Instant Discount (deducts from cart subtotal), Free Gothic Artifact Gift (enclosed in packaging), and VIP Order Upgrade.
  • Order Sync: Applied discounts and claimed reward gifts automatically flow into customer orders and display with colored badges in the Admin Orders panel.

✦Zero-Refresh Live Admin Engine & Tombstones

  • Live In-Place State: All admin operations (updating order status, adjusting stock steppers, editing products, and adding reviews) update DOM state instantly without full-page reloads.
  • Tombstone Protection: Soft-deletion tombstones (gn_deleted_orders, gn_deleted_products, gn_deleted_reviews) guarantee deleted items never reappear when switching devices or refreshing.
